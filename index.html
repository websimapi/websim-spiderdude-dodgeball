Spiderdude Dodgeball

"MY TARANTULA SENSES! THEY BURN!"

How to Play

  • Click and hold to shoot a rope from the nearest hand to the ceiling
  • Release to drop the rope
  • Press 'A' and 'D' to move left and right
  • Watch out for the flying balls! You have 10 lives
  • Hold 'S' to activate tarantula senses!
